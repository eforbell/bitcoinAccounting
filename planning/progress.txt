- **2026-02-13 - Feature-12: Transaction Editor**
  Added transaction detail, edit, soft-delete, restore, and show-deleted flows backed by ledger soft-delete columns.

- **2026-02-19 - Feature-13: Bitcoin Accounting Rebrand and Compatibility Migration**
  Completed the staged `crypto*` to `bitcoin*` rebrand with compatibility shims, canonical entrypoints, and migration docs.
